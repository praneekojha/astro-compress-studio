      About AstroPress

      Pranik Ojha is the founder of AstroPress. The goal is simple: free, professional tools for photographers and sky watchers.

      Privacy

      Your photos and videos are processed on your device and are never uploaded by AstroPress. The only network request is the first video compression, which downloads the open-source encoder (ffmpeg.wasm) from the jsDelivr CDN. Your theme choice is saved in your browser. AstroPress sets no cookies and has no accounts or analytics. You keep full rights to every file you process.

      FAQ

      Is it really lossless?

      PNG output is lossless. WebP, JPEG and video are lossy, but high settings look the same to the eye. Smaller files always mean some data is discarded.

      Are my files uploaded?

      No. Everything is processed locally in your browser.

      Why is video slow?

      Your own device does the work, so long or very large videos take time and memory.

      Why does my stack look blurry?

      Photos are not auto-aligned. Use a tripod and keep the framing identical.
