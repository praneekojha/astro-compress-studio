Frequently asked questions

Is it really lossless?

PNG output is lossless. WebP, JPEG and video are lossy, but high settings look the same to the eye. Smaller files always mean some data is discarded.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything is processed locally on your device.

Why is video slow?

Video is compressed by your own device, so long or very large videos take time and memory.
