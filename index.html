FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No limits, watermarks or sign-up.

Why does it take as long as the video?

For compatibility with Safari and Chrome, the video is re-recorded at normal speed. A 2-minute clip takes about 2 minutes.

Why is my result MP4 or WebM?

Safari and recent Chrome save MP4. Older browsers may save WebM.

Can it make a video bigger?

No. Choosing a size larger than your video keeps the original size.
